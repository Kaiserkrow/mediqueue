The Problem

Current Clinic Challenges

Manual queue handling creates confusion, delays, and privacy risks in school clinic environments.

Unstructured Queue

Manual, first-come-first-serve with no system support leads to disorganized waiting and inefficiency.

Informal Priority

Urgent cases are handled without system support, creating perceived unfairness among waiting students.

No Queue Visibility

Students have no insight into their queue position, leading to frustration and repeated inquiries.

Privacy Concerns

Sensitive patient information is accessed without role-based restrictions in school environments.

Registration Barriers

Systems requiring pre-registration can delay or block urgent emergency care for students in crisis.

Delayed Response

Lack of system coordination slows down clinic response times, especially during peak hours.

Key Features

Everything a School Clinic Needs

Six core capabilities that make MediQueue the complete solution for school patient flow management.

Core
Structured Queue Management

Default First-Come, First-Served ordering with a fully organized and trackable queue system for all visits.

Priority
Priority Override Mechanism

Walk-In Emergency Handling

How It Works

Two Workflows, One System

MediQueue handles both standard visits and walk-in emergencies seamlessly.

1
Student Logs In

Student accesses MediQueue and submits their visit details and symptoms through the self-registration form.

2
System Assigns Queue Number

MediQueue automatically assigns a queue number and initial "Waiting" status to the patient.

3
Placed in FCFS Order

Patient joins the queue in first-come, first-served order and can monitor their position in real time.

4
Called & Marked Complete

Clinic staff calls the next patient, updates status to "Called", then "Completed" after the visit.

!
Student Proceeds Directly to Clinic

In an emergency, no prior system registration is required — the student goes directly to the clinic for immediate care.

2
Staff Provides Immediate Assistance

Clinic staff attends to the patient without any system delays — emergency care is never gated by registration.

3
Quick Entry Mode Activated

Staff logs the patient via Quick Entry Mode — a rapid input form designed for speed under pressure.

4
Priority Status Assigned

Patient is assigned emergency priority and placed at the top of the queue automatically.
